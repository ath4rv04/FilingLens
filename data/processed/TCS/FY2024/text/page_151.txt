5.	
Has the entity implemented a mechanism for Zero Liquid Discharge? If yes, provide details of its coverage and 
implementation74.
	
Yes, TCS has achieved zero liquid discharge across most of its owned campuses, except those where there are operational 
constraints. TCS optimizes water consumption through conservation, sewage treatment and reuse, and rainwater harvesting. 
All newer campuses have been designed for treatment and recycling of sewage and rainwater harvesting.
