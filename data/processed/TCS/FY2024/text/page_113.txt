The Members who have a claim on above dividends and/or shares are requested to follow the below process:
	
	
1.	
Submit self-attested copies of documents provided in IEPF 5 helpkit, which is available on IEPF website (www.iepf.gov.in) 
to the Company / Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA).
	
	
2.	
After verification of the aforesaid documents submitted, Company will issue an entitlement letter.
	
	
3.	
File Form IEPF-5 on IEPF website and send self-attested copies of IEPF-5 form along with the acknowledgement (SRN), 
Indemnity bond and entitlement letter to Company.
	
	
4.	
On receipt of the physical documents mentioned above, Company will submit e-Verification report, for further 
processing by the IEPF Authority.
	
	
Members are requested to note that no claims shall lie against the Company in respect of the dividend/shares transferred to IEPF.
